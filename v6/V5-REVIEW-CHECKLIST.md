# V5 visual review checklist

## Temporal stability
- [ ] Shadow edges do not crawl during the fastest camera move.
- [ ] No world-space shadow pattern becomes visible as a surface texture.
- [ ] Fine grain does not shimmer or form obvious screen-space bands.
- [ ] Water caustics animate without sudden jumps or harsh aliasing.

## Image quality
- [ ] Bronze highlights retain engraved detail and do not clip to white.
- [ ] Stone texture reads as subtle surface variation, not noisy bump.
- [ ] Water has a clear depth cue and does not resemble a flat cyan panel.
- [ ] Dark mechanism interiors remain readable without looking artificially lifted.
- [ ] Foreground, mechanism and background remain visually separated.
- [ ] Depth of field does not blur the moving mechanism at the moment of explanation.
- [ ] Motion blur follows the camera move without ghost trails at object edges.

## Editorial and sound
- [ ] Every major mechanical action begins when the narration describes it.
- [ ] No component moves before its causal trigger.
- [ ] Captions are correctly timed, spelled and safely inside the frame.
- [ ] Sound effects support the action without masking narration.
- [ ] Cuts preserve spatial orientation and mechanism continuity.

## Release gate
- [ ] Watch the complete film at normal speed with sound.
- [ ] Review every chapter transition and every fast camera move frame-by-frame.
- [ ] Confirm the final encoded file's duration, resolution, frame rate and audio sync.
- [ ] Keep the existing approved/WIP render until the new master passes review.
